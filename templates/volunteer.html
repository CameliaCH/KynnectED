Become a mentor

Work directly with a refugee student through weekly personalised tutoring.

Apply to Mentor

What you’ll do

Plan learning activities, explain concepts, and help a student practise in the subjects where they need support.

Your commitment

Make time for consistent weekly sessions, preparation, and communication with your pairing. Agree on a schedule you can reliably maintain.

Who can apply?

Student volunteers around the world who are confident in their subject, patient, and willing to learn alongside someone else.

Getting started

Complete your profile and availability. Expect mentor vetting and an interview before a coordinator arranges a suitable pairing.

Safeguarding

Respect student privacy, use programme communication channels, and follow safeguarding expectations. Raise concerns with a coordinator.
