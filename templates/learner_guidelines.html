1. Use KynnectED chat only. Message your mentor through KynnectED. Do not move your conversations to WhatsApp, social media, or other apps.
  2. Keep personal details private. Do not share phone numbers, email addresses, home addresses, social media accounts, or passwords.
  3. Allow up to one week to be matched. We will message/email you once your mentor pairing has been confirmed. You can also log in and check your dashboard for updates.
  4. Attend your weekly lessons. Use your pairing page to chat with your mentor and agree on a time. Your mentor will schedule the lesson, and you can join using the Zoom link on your dashboard. If you cannot attend, let your mentor know through KynnectED chat.
  5. Watch lessons again later. Lessons are recorded for safeguarding and learning. Once your mentor uploads the recording, you can watch it under Finished Lessons. Recordings may take up to one week to appear.
  6. Be respectful and ask for help. Bring any questions, homework, or topics you would like support with. If anything makes you feel uncomfortable or unsafe, tell a trusted adult and contact the KynnectED team.
