Your quick learner guide

Welcome! Please read these simple steps before you join.

  • Use our chat only. Message your mentor through KynnectED. Do not move chats to WhatsApp, social media, or other apps.
  • Keep personal details private. Do not exchange phone numbers, email addresses, home addresses, social media accounts, or passwords.
  • Allow about one week for a mentor. Log in and check your dashboard for your pairing. If you are still waiting after a week, contact the KynnectED team.
  • Join your lessons. Open your pairing to chat and agree on a time. Your mentor schedules the lesson. Use the Zoom link on your dashboard to join on time. If you cannot attend, tell your mentor in our chat.
  • Watch again later. Lessons are recorded. After your mentor uploads a recording, you can watch it in Finished lessons. Uploads may take up to a week.
  • Be kind and ask for help. Bring your questions or homework. If anything makes you uncomfortable, tell a trusted adult or contact our team.
