Getting Started

Once you have been matched with a mentee:

  1. Introduce yourself

    Open your pairing and send your mentee a message briefly introducing yourself.

  2. Understand what they need help with

    Ask which subject they would like to work on, their current level or grade, and whether there are any specific topics they need help with.

  3. Find a suitable time

    Ask when they are available and agree on a date and time for your first lesson.

  4. Schedule the lesson

    Create the session through KynnectED and attach the relevant Zoom meeting link.

  5. Prepare for the lesson

    Use the information your mentee has given you to prepare suitable questions, explanations, or resources before the session.

  6. Record and upload your session

    Make sure each session is recorded and upload the recording to KynnectED after the lesson. Once the recording is uploaded, your attendance for that session will be marked automatically.

Remember: Keep all communication with your mentee within KynnectED and follow the Mentor Code of Conduct at all times.
