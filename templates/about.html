About KynnectED

Our mission is to provide personalised one-to-one mentoring for refugee students across Malaysia.

Our story

KynnectED connects refugee students in Malaysia with student mentors around the world, providing personalised one-to-one academic support to help address gaps caused by limited access to educational resources. Our aim is to give every student greater access to the individual support and opportunities they need to learn and grow.

The programme provides regular academic support tailored to each student’s needs.
