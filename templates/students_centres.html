For refugee students

Meet one-to-one each week with a student mentor who can help you practise, work through difficult topics, and build confidence.

Sign up

What can I learn?

English, Mathematics, Science, Languages, and Study Skills. Tell us where you need support so we can look for a suitable mentor.

How does matching work?

Share your learning needs and availability when you sign up. A coordinator reviews your information and arranges a pairing. Your connection will appear on your dashboard.

What do I need?

A device with internet access, a place to join online sessions, and regular weekly availability. Your learning centre or guardian can help you get started.

Does it cost anything?

No. KynnectED mentoring is free for students.

For refugee learning centres

We help centres arrange individual academic support alongside their existing lessons.

What KynnectED provides

  • Volunteer student mentors
  • Personalised one-to-one sessions
  • A matching and scheduling platform
  • Progress tracking through session history and hours
  • Educational resources as our library grows

What we ask from centres

  • Help identifying suitable students
  • Guardian consent before participation
  • Basic learning needs and availability
  • Feedback to help improve the programme
